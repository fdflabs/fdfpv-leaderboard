What this counts

Nothing here is about you

Every number on this page is a count. The simulator tells this board that a session started, that laps were flown, and which aircraft and input were used. The board adds one to a daily total and keeps nothing else.

No cookie is set. No address is stored: the edge in front of the site names a country from it, and only the two letter code arrives here. New or returning is worked out by your browser from a date it keeps for itself, and no identifier ever leaves it. A sponsor link is remembered as one word for thirty days, so the board can say which poster somebody walked past, never who they are. Days are UTC.
